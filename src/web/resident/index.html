DOH Dengue Prevention

Evidence-based guidance for Albay households

Know dengue. Protect your home.

Search and destroy

Empty, scrub and cover water containers.

Self-protection

Use insect repellent, wear long sleeves, and screen windows.

Seek early consultation

Consult your RHU or hospital if fever lasts more than 2 days.

Support fogging where needed

Cooperate during targeted BHW larviciding and fogging operations.

Sustain hydration

Drink water, oral rehydration salts (ORS), or clear broths regularly.

Febrile Phase (Days 1–3)

Sudden high fever, severe headache, pain behind the eyes, and muscle/joint aches.

Critical Phase (Days 3–7)

Fever may drop while warning signs appear: severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, or mucosal bleeding.

Aedes aegypti habits

Bites mostly during early morning and late afternoon. Breeds in clean, stagnant household water.

7-day egg-to-adult cycle

Eggs laid on damp container walls hatch when rain refills them and become biting adults within a week.

Myth: Dengue mosquitoes only breed in dirty canals

Fact: Aedes mosquitoes prefer clean, still water in drums, flower vases, dish racks, and roof gutters.

FAQ: Which pain reliever is safe?

Use Paracetamol only as prescribed. Avoid aspirin, ibuprofen, and mefenamic acid as they increase bleeding risk.
